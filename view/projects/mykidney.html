My Kidney

My Kidney? "My Kidney is a personal project designed to demonstrate how Artificial Intelligence can be applied in mobile health apps. This app guides users through selecting symptoms, then provides a possible diagnosis and advice—like a digital consultation assistant."

View on App Store GitHub Repository

The Goal

The primary objective of the "Save Your Kidney" application is to empower users to proactively manage and monitor their renal health through an intuitive, all-in-one digital platform. By unifying secure user onboarding, curated educational modules on kidney disease prevention, and interactive personalized health dashboards, the app aims to simplify complex medical information and make certified specialist guidance accessible, ultimately helping individuals detect potential kidney issues early and adopt healthier daily lifestyle habits.

The Challenge

The main challenge lies in transforming dense, intimidating health data into an engaging, user-friendly UI/UX experience that builds user trust without causing undue anxiety. Designing a seamless interface that balances robust data privacy during authentication, organizes multifaceted health content clearly across various device screens, and delivers actionable, personalized insights requires a precise design approach that caters to diverse health literacy levels while maintaining high visual clarity and functional reliability.

Visual Nutrition Tracking

Built natively with SwiftUI Charts, this feature empowers users to effortlessly monitor their dietary intake through clean, interactive visual data. By transforming daily food logs into clear graphical insights, users can analyze their consumption patterns and track long-term nutrition trends at a glance.

sync

Real-Time Sync

Powered by a high-performance Vapor Swift backend, the application ensures instantaneous data synchronization across the entire user ecosystem. Every dietary log or profile update entered on an iPhone is immediately reflected across connected endpoints, delivering a seamless and responsive cross-device experience.

lock

Secure Local Storage

Designed with an offline-first architecture, all sensitive health metrics and personal consumption logs are encrypted and persisted locally using Core Data. This setup guarantees zero latency and immediate offline accessibility while upholding the highest standards of user data privacy and security.
